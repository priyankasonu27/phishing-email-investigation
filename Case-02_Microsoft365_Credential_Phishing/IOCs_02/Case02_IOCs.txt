Case 02 – Microsoft 365 Credential Phishing
IOC (Indicators of Compromise)

1. Sender Email
security@micros0ft-account.test

2. Sender Domain
micros0ft-account.test

3. Reply-To Email
verify-account@account-security.test

4. Reply-To Domain
account-security.test

5. Source IP Address
203.0.113.42

6. Suspicious URL
https://login-microsoft365.account-security.test/verify

7. Suspicious Domain
login-microsoft365.account-security.test

8. Message-ID
<m365-20260903-091422@micros0ft-account.test>

9. Subject
Your Microsoft 365 password will expire today

10. Look-alike Domain Indicator
micros0ft-account.test
("0" used instead of "o" in Microsoft)

11. Authentication Indicators
SPF: FAIL
DKIM: NONE
DMARC: FAIL
DMARC Policy: p=reject

Assessment:
The above indicators were extracted from the synthetic phishing email
used in this lab. The email contains multiple phishing indicators,
including a look-alike sender domain, mismatched Reply-To domain,
failed email authentication, urgency-based messaging, and a suspicious
account verification URL.

Note:
This is a controlled cybersecurity training artifact using .test
domains and a documentation/test IP address. No real malicious activity
is being attributed to these indicators.
